04 · Spin-in-place cards — spaced, pinned

All 5 cards spiral around the central column — as they descend, each sits a step further around the helix (front/back depth included), not a flat zigzag. Each also spins about its own axis; the card the scroll "lines up with" faces forward (bright, moving-border) — the rest are edge-on & dim. The facing card stays where it's pinned (off-center is fine). Two frozen states below show how the facing card changes as you scroll.

05 · Card hover treatment

On hover, a card snaps to face forward, freezes its spin, and lights up: brighter moving border (with a single amber spark in the gradient), scale-up, and a glow halo. No scanner sweep. Same treatment for metric/experience cards.
