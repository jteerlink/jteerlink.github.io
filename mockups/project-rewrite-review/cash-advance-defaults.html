Suggested rewrite

The sharper story

This case study should read less like a notebook summary and more like a credit decisioning system.

The project predicts which cash-advance customers are most likely to default by converting account behavior into stable, interpretable risk signals. Rather than leaning on demographic proxies or one-off borrower labels, the modeling work centers on liquidity, volatility, overdraft behavior, income regularity, and recent cash-flow pressure.

The final model uses XGBoost because the data is noisy, partially sparse, and likely non-linear. Optuna tuning improved AUC to 0.7201 and Gini to 0.4401, with meaningful separation across deciles. The highest-value next step is not another algorithm swap; it is deeper transaction-level features, score calibration, and policy design with credit stakeholders.

The rewrite should make the business value explicit: faster risk triage, more consistent underwriting, and a path from raw probabilities to production-ready risk tiers.
