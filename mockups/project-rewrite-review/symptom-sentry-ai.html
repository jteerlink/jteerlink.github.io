Suggested rewrite

The sharper story

The product is strongest when framed as a safe analysis workflow, not just an image classifier with a UI.

Symptom Sentry AI gives users a guided way to capture and review throat or ear images for early infection signals. The model output is intentionally simple — normal or abnormal with confidence — because the real product challenge is not showing off model complexity. It is making the experience understandable, secure, and useful enough that a user can take the next responsible step.

The application combines a responsive frontend, TensorFlow.js inference, a Node/Express backend, PostgreSQL-backed analysis history, and hardened authentication. That architecture lets the app separate sensitive user identity and history from the image-analysis flow while still giving users continuity across sessions.

The improved narrative should emphasize product judgment: careful UX around medical uncertainty, privacy-conscious inference, clear result visualization, and a security model appropriate for a health-adjacent app.
